# Module 6: Integrations & Interoperability

To provide a truly holistic view of wellbeing, Helix operates as part of a connected ecosystem. It seamlessly integrates with external tools to enrich its contextual understanding.

## Technical Foundation: Universal Interoperability (MCP)

Helix adopts a microservices-based architecture and a **Universal Interoperability Protocol (MCP)**. This functions as a "universal adapter," reducing integration complexity from M×N to M+N.

## Integration Categories

* **Wearables & Health Apps**: Direct integration with Fitbit, Garmin, Apple Health, and Google Fit for activity, sleep, and biometric data.
* **Productivity Tools**: Syncing with Google Calendar and project management apps (e.g., Asana) to understand workload and deadlines.
