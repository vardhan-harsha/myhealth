# Module 4: Community & Social Wellbeing

Social connection is a critical component of holistic health. Helix uses AI to facilitate meaningful interactions, turning the app into a "digital placemaking" hub and a powerful defense against modern loneliness.

## Features

* **AI-Powered Peer Matching**: Suggests connections based on similar goals, interests, or professional roles ("Buddy Program").
* **Community Events**: Functionality to create and join group meditations, fitness challenges, or discussion groups.
* **Shared Storytelling**: AI-identified common themes across user-shared narratives (with consent) to inspire others.
* **Virtual Mastermind Groups**: Focused sub-communities like "Vegetarian Fat Loss" or "Beginner Strength Training" to foster peer accountability.

## Gamification & Recognition

The system celebrates leading indicators of success (consistency) rather than just lagging indicators (weight lost):

* **Streaks**: Badge or XP for 30-day calorie logging.
* **Milestones**: Personal records in compound lifts or major weight goals reached.
* **Consistency**: XP for hitting protein targets for a full month.
