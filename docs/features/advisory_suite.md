# Module 5: AI Advisory & Personalization Suite

This module delivers a "human-like" AI experience, transforming Helix from a simple tool into a trusted coach. It offers personalized, expert-level advice to help users navigate challenges and de-risk decisions.

## AI Coach Personas (GSM Persona Mods)

Users can select a coaching style that best resonates with their personality:

| Persona Archetype | Coaching Style | Example Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Scientist** | Data-driven and analytical, focusing on biological mechanisms and evidence-based practices. | *"Your weight has stalled. The data suggests increasing your daily step count as it's less metabolically stressful than reducing food further."* |
| **The Motivator** | Empathetic and encouraging, focusing on building confidence and celebrating small wins. | *"I noticed your squat weight went up even with lower sleep. That shows grit! Let's prioritize rest this weekend to lock in those gains."* |

These advisors leverage the full spectrum of tracked data to provide context-aware feedback, making expert guidance scalable 24/7.
